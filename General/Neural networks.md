## How does it work:
AI works thanks to neural networks, a complex of neurons that work together to solve problems, just like our brains' neurons, but instead of working with electric waves it works with math, a lot of math.
explained easily, it works like this:

- First of all, we have a structure of neurons where we may have more than one layer, the first layer is the data input that passes those to the computational part of the network. The middle layers are called "hidden", here everything gets calculated, Finally there's the last layer, where we output a prediction, classification or result based on the type of data we're handling;

- Every neuron in the previous layer is connected to every one of the next, every connection is measured by a weight that, in fact, a (not so) random value that measures the importance of that connection;

- With specific functions those weights (and biases, other values important to get the output), took all the way down to the last layer, gives us a result or other type of output.

It's a lot more complicated than that, but for now the basics will be enough.
